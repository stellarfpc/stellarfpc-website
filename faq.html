FIRE SAFETY PLAN HELP

Fire Safety Plan Questions, Answered.

Clear answers to common questions about Fire Safety Plans, updates, training and the review process in Ontario.

01 - Getting Started
How do I know if my property needs a Fire Safety Plan?

Fire Safety Plan requirements depend on the building's occupancy, size, use and other conditions under the Ontario Fire Code.

If you are unsure, send us the property address and a short description of the building and we can help you identify the appropriate next step.

What information do you need to prepare a Fire Safety Plan?

We normally start with the building address, occupancy or use, available floor plans and any existing Fire Safety Plan.

Depending on the property, we may also need information about the fire alarm, sprinkler, standpipe, emergency power or other building fire safety systems.

Do you need to visit the building?

Not every project requires the same approach.

A site visit may be recommended when existing information is incomplete, the building has changed, or the fire protection systems and layout need to be confirmed.

How long does it take to prepare a Fire Safety Plan?

Many standard projects can typically be prepared within about 1-2 weeks after we receive the required building information.

Rush service may be available for qualifying projects, depending on scope, document availability and scheduling.

02 - Preparation & Review
What does StellarFPC include in a Fire Safety Plan?

The exact content depends on the building, but a plan may include emergency procedures, supervisory staff responsibilities, fire drill procedures, fire protection system information and floor plan details.

The document is prepared specifically for the building rather than using a one-size-fits-all template.

Can you update an existing Fire Safety Plan instead of creating a new one?

Yes.

We can review and update existing plans when there have been renovations, occupancy changes, system changes, management changes or when the plan requires its regular review.

Do you submit the Fire Safety Plan to the fire department?

We can support the submission and review process where required.

The review and approval process is controlled by the authority having jurisdiction, and timelines vary by municipality and project complexity.

What happens if the fire department asks for revisions?

We can review the comments and assist with reasonable revisions related to the agreed Fire Safety Plan scope.

Our goal is to keep the review process organized and respond clearly to technical comments.

Can you guarantee Fire Department approval?

Final approval is determined by the local authority having jurisdiction, so no consultant should control or guarantee that decision.

What we can provide is professional preparation, clear documentation and support during the review process.

How often should a Fire Safety Plan be reviewed?

Fire Safety Plans should be kept current and reviewed whenever building conditions or operations change.

The Ontario Fire Code also requires applicable plans to be reviewed at intervals of no more than 12 months.

03 - Training & Ongoing Compliance
Do you provide Fire Safety Plan training for building staff?

Yes.

We provide practical training for supervisory staff and building management so they understand emergency procedures, fire drill requirements and their responsibilities under the Fire Safety Plan.

Is Fire Safety Plan training included with a new plan?

Qualifying new Fire Safety Plan clients may receive one complimentary basic on-site Fire Safety Plan training session.

Training scope, location, attendee limits and scheduling are subject to the project terms.

Who should attend Fire Safety Plan training?

Training is generally intended for people who have responsibilities under the Fire Safety Plan.

This may include property managers, superintendents, building operators, security staff and other designated supervisory personnel.

Can you help with annual Fire Safety Plan reviews?

Yes.

We can review existing plans, update building information and help keep emergency procedures, supervisory staff information and documentation current.

Can you help before a Fire Inspection Order is issued?

Yes.

Our proactive fire safety compliance service can help identify Fire Code or documentation concerns before they develop into larger compliance issues.

Can you help if I already received a Fire Inspection Order?

Yes.

We can review the listed deficiencies, explain the requirements and help develop a practical compliance approach within our scope of services.
