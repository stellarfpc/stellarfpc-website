Does Your Building Need a Fire Safety Plan?

Many residential, commercial, assembly and industrial buildings in Ontario require a Fire Safety Plan under the Ontario Fire Code.

A professional Fire Safety Plan should reflect the actual building, occupancy, fire protection systems, emergency procedures and supervisory staff responsibilities.

If you need a Fire Safety Plan, StellarFPC is ready to help.

Our team brings practical fire prevention and Fire Code experience to the preparation of professional, building-specific Fire Safety Plans. We focus on quick response, clear documentation and practical support throughout the review process.
