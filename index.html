Ontario Fire Code

Does Your Building Need a Fire Safety Plan?

Fire Safety Plans are required for many buildings and premises under Section 2.8 of the Ontario Fire Code. Requirements depend on factors including occupancy type, occupant load, building height and the activities taking place in the building.

Not sure whether your property requires a Fire Safety Plan? Send us the building address and a brief description of the property. We can help identify the appropriate next step.
