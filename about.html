Professional Fire Safety Consulting. Personal Service.

Stellar Fire Protection & Code Consulting is an Ontario-based fire safety consulting company serving property owners, property managers, businesses and project teams. Our company now includes a Limited Licence holder, adding engineering stamp capability and professional fire protection engineering services within the authorized scope of the licence. We provide Fire Safety Plan preparation and training, annual fire alarm system testing, proactive fire safety compliance support, Fire Inspection Order solutions and fire sprinkler system design support.

Our goal is to help clients understand their fire safety responsibilities, keep required documentation current and address fire protection concerns before they become more difficult or costly to manage. We focus on clear communication, practical recommendations and straightforward project delivery from the first request to the final report or submission.

Our qualifications combine Fire Safety Plan knowledge, Fire Code compliance experience, fire protection system design support, CFAA-certified fire alarm testing capability and Limited Licence holder engineering support. We understand that each building is different, so our work reflects the actual occupancy, fire alarm and sprinkler systems, emergency procedures, supervisory staff responsibilities and site conditions.

Our value is practical fire safety service with professional care, quick response, fair pricing and building-specific attention. Whether a client needs a new Fire Safety Plan, staff training, annual fire alarm testing, help with a Fire Inspection Order, proactive compliance review, sprinkler design support or engineering stamp services, our priority is to make the process clear, efficient and useful for real building operations.
