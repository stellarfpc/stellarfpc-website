More Than Document Preparation

Understanding the Building Behind the Fire Safety Plan

Preparing an effective Fire Safety Plan often requires understanding much more than emergency procedures.

The building may include fire alarm systems, sprinkler systems, standpipe systems, exits, fire separations, emergency power and other fire and life safety features.

Our fire protection and Fire Code background allows us to review the Fire Safety Plan in the context of the overall building.

This broader understanding helps us communicate more effectively with building owners, property managers, contractors and other project stakeholders when additional fire protection issues are identified.
