Professional Fire Safety Consulting. Personal Service.

Stellar Fire Protection & Code Consulting is a small Ontario-based fire safety consulting company serving property owners, property managers, businesses and project teams. Our main focus is Fire Safety Plan preparation, updates and training, supported by practical Fire Code and fire protection system experience.

As a small consulting company, our priority is simple: provide professional work, responsive communication and practical solutions at a fair and competitive price. Clients work directly with our team, allowing us to respond quickly, understand the building and keep the project straightforward without unnecessary layers of administration.

Our advantage is the combination of Fire Safety Plan knowledge and broader fire protection experience. We understand that a Fire Safety Plan is not just a document. It needs to reflect the actual building, occupancy, fire alarm and sprinkler systems, emergency procedures and supervisory staff responsibilities. This allows us to prepare clear, building-specific documentation that is practical for both building management and the review process.

Our core values are professionalism, responsiveness, quality and client-focused service. Whether a client needs a new Fire Safety Plan, an update to an existing plan, staff training, assistance with a Fire Inspection Order or fire sprinkler design support, our goal is to make the process clear, efficient and practical from beginning to end.
