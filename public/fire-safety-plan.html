StellarFPC provides new Fire Safety Plan preparation, existing plan updates, annual reviews and supervisory staff training for residential, commercial, assembly, industrial and mixed-use properties throughout Ontario.

Each Fire Safety Plan is prepared around the specific building, occupancy, fire protection systems, emergency procedures and supervisory staff responsibilities.

Prepared with consideration of the applicable Ontario Fire Code requirements and the actual conditions of the building.

Proactive Compliance

Proactive Fire Safety Compliance Program

Our proactive compliance program is designed to help property managers and building owners identify Fire Code issues before they develop into more serious compliance concerns or inspection orders.

Why Be Proactive?

Regular reviews can help property owners identify documentation, maintenance and Fire Code concerns earlier and plan corrective action before issues become more difficult or costly to manage.

Program services may include:

  • Annual Fire Safety Plan Review
  • Annual Fire Safety Plan Training
  • Proactive Fire Code Review
  • Review of Available Maintenance Records
  • Fire Safety Documentation Review
  • Deficiency Recommendations
