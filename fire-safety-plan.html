Preparation & Review

Fire Safety Plan Preparation & Review

We prepare new Fire Safety Plans and update existing plans for residential, commercial, assembly, industrial and mixed-use properties throughout Ontario.

Each Fire Safety Plan is developed around the specific building, occupancy, fire protection systems, emergency procedures and supervisory staff responsibilities.

Prepared with consideration of the applicable requirements of the Ontario Fire Code and the specific conditions of the building.

Our services may include:

  • New Fire Safety Plan Preparation
  • Existing Fire Safety Plan Updates
  • Annual Fire Safety Plan Reviews
  • Emergency Procedures
  • Building & Fire Protection System Information
  • Floor Plan / Schematic Information
  • Fire Department Submission Support
Revision Support

Proactive Compliance

Proactive Fire Safety Compliance Program

Our proactive compliance program is designed to help property managers and building owners identify Fire Code issues before they develop into more serious compliance concerns or inspection orders.

Why Be Proactive?

Regular reviews can help property owners identify documentation, maintenance and Fire Code concerns earlier and plan corrective action before issues become more difficult or costly to manage.

Program services may include:

  • Annual Fire Safety Plan Review
  • Annual Fire Safety Plan Training
